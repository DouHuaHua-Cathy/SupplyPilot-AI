name: SupplyPilot product scope
description: Product purpose and the explicit Stage 1 boundary for SupplyPilot AI.
---

SupplyPilot AI is a prototype for Malaysian small businesses to compare purchase orders with supplier messages and catch quantity or delivery discrepancies. Stage 1 uses fictional data and deterministic, rule-based extraction; it does not connect to WhatsApp or use AI, external APIs, authentication, or a cloud database.

**Why:** The user defined this as a focused SDC Hackathon 2026 Domain 2 prototype and explicitly deferred integrations and production services.

**How to apply:** Keep early iterations local and transparent. Do not add live messaging, AI extraction, authentication, or persistence unless the user asks to expand the scope.
